# AsciiEQ

**A real-time equalizer, effects chain, mixer and visualizer for Windows — drawn in retro pixel-art and ASCII.**
Free to start; Pro and Studio add the deep stuff. Download at **[asciieq.com](https://asciieq.com)**.

## Open source here

| Repo | What it is |
|---|---|
| [**scene-studio**](https://github.com/AsciiEQ/scene-studio) | Design `.asciiscene` backgrounds that move with your music: the scene format, 13 patterns, the renderer, the editor and a standalone `SceneStudio.exe`. MIT. |
| [**discord-caption-bot**](https://github.com/AsciiEQ/discord-caption-bot) | Self-hosted live captions for Discord calls, labelled per speaker, for AsciiEQ. MIT. |

Made a scene? Share it in the **[community gallery](https://asciieq.com/scenes)** — it goes into AsciiEQ with one click
(Themes → Import…) and can be your Windows wallpaper or a live desktop.
